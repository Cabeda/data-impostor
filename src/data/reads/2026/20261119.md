## [Is organic farming better for the environment than conventional farming? by Hannah Ritchie](https://ourworldindata.org/is-organic-farming-better-for-the-environment-than-conventional-farming)

- If the world were to move to a 100% organic farming system, we’d have to convert far more forest and natural habitat into farmland. This could ultimately work against our intention to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and preserve biodiversity.
- A future with less agricultural land but more organic farming would only be possible with widespread shifts to more plant-based, less land-hungry diets and large reductions in food waste.
